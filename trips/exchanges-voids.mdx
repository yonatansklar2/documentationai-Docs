Trips change. Yaalago has dedicated flows for the common post-booking scenarios.

## Trip replacement

Replace a trip with updated GDS data when the itinerary is reissued, keeping the record and its history intact.

## Voided trips

Void a trip while **keeping the fee** - useful when a ticket is voided within the void window but your service fee still applies.
