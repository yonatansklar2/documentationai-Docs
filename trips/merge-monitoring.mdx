Real trips are rarely a single clean PNR. Yaalago helps you assemble them and stay ahead of changes.

## Multi-booking merge

Combine multiple PNRs, hotel reservations, and car rentals into **one unified trip record**. On merged trips, each passenger on each flight clearly shows **both** their agency confirmation code (PNR) and the airline confirmation code - visible in client links, PDFs, emails, previews, and manual entry.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a trip" icon="map">
    Open the trip you want to build up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add bookings" icon="git-merge">
    Add additional PNRs or reservations to merge them into the single trip.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Void-period countdowns

Every booking gets an automatic **countdown timer** for its void deadline, with visual warnings before the window closes - so you never miss a free cancellation window.

## Schedule-change & cancellation monitoring

<Callout kind="info">
  **Any flight entered into Yaalago** - via AI booking or manual entry, not only GDS Sync - is automatically tracked for schedule changes and cancellations. When an airline moves a time or cancels a segment, your agency is notified, so you are on top of it before the customer even realizes. This extends change monitoring to non-GDS and points bookings.
</Callout>

## Smart trip management

Tasks can be linked to specific trips and segments. When a flight changes, related tasks update automatically, and deadlines are timezone-aware. Hotel check-ins, car pickups, and flights appear in the correct timeline order.
