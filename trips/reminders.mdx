Yaalago sends automated, agency-branded emails around each trip so nothing falls through the cracks and your customers feel well taken care of.

## Automated post-booking emails

When a trip is created, three emails fire automatically:

1. A vendor notice
2. Agent commission details
3. A client trip confirmation

## The 36-hour pre-journey reminder

A beautifully designed, agency-branded email fires automatically **36 hours before any flight's departure**. It includes per-passenger confirmation codes, a check-in link, agency contact details, and local weather.

### Two modes per agency

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Agency-only (default)" icon="inbox">
    Lands in the agent's inbox so they can review and optionally forward it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Agency + customer" icon="send">
    Sends directly to the traveler too - fully automated, no agent review needed.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Callout kind="tip">
  You can **preview** the reminder from any trip page to see exactly what it will look like - branding, confirmation codes, check-in link, and all - before it sends. When sent to the customer, it lands before the airline's own check-in notification, making your agency the first touchpoint of the journey.
</Callout>
