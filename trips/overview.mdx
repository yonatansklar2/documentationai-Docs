---
title: Importing Trips
description: Bring trips into Yaalago from Sabre, Amadeus, a manual entry, or an AI confirmation import.
---

A **trip** is the central record in Yaalago - it holds the itinerary, passengers, pricing, payments, and tasks. There are several ways to create one.

## Ways to import

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Sabre" icon="plane">
    Display the full PNR in Sabre (using your PF key), copy it, then in Yaalago click **Add Trip** and paste. Yaalago extracts passengers, segments, and fares. The [Yaalago Porter](/gds/porter) extension can capture the PNR for you in one click.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Amadeus" icon="plane">
    Copy the Amadeus PNR and paste it into **Add Trip**. Multi-segment itineraries and commission tracking are supported.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Manual" icon="edit">
    Add any trip manually - useful for award trips, third-party bookings, or anything not in a GDS. You can mark a manual segment as **open** for reporting.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="AI import" icon="sparkles">
    Drop a confirmation PDF or email from any provider and let AI build the trip. See [Universal Confirmation Import](/proposals/confirmation-import).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Award & third-party bookings

Yaalago handles award trips and third-party bookings, including subagency bookings with split fees. When a subagency booking is submitted, three emails are auto-generated: a ticket confirmation, an agent commission note, and a trip confirmation.

## Per-trip details

- **Seats, meals, and baggage** per passenger per segment
- **Trip allowances** for seating and baggage
- **Payments** recorded on the Edit Trip page and associated automatically

<Callout kind="tip">
  Every imported trip automatically creates or merges the [client record](/clients/overview) from the traveler details.
</Callout>
