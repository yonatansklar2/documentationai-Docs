---
title: Importing Trips
description: Bring trips into Yaalago from Sabre, Amadeus, a manual entry, or an AI confirmation import.
---

A **trip** is the central record in Yaalago - it holds the itinerary, passengers, pricing, payments, and tasks. Import a full PNR from Sabre or Amadeus, add a booking to an existing trip, enter a trip manually, or use [AI confirmation import](/proposals/confirmation-import). Every imported trip automatically creates or merges the [client record](/clients/overview).

## Import a trip from the GDS

<Steps>
  <Step title="Show all PNR sections">
    In your GDS, run the **show all** command to open every section of the PNR (works for both Sabre and Amadeus).

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_747ba6ad-66ef-4270-8f8c-17c8fcdb5b15.gif" alt="Show all" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the PNR data">
    Highlight and copy the full PNR to your clipboard.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_c899e046-14cb-4589-9572-dae29b25c465.gif" alt="Copy PNR" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste into Add Trip">
    In **Trips**, open the GDS add-trip function, paste the copied data, and convert it.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_68fe9c18-2136-4a0d-8bb7-f32b2f94366d.gif" alt="Add trip" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm flight and agent details">
    Verify the flight dates, booking agent, sales agent, and agency, and assign the correct flights to the ticket numbers.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_23b47f97-002e-400d-9ba9-69f7830b8eac.gif" alt="Confirm details" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review accounting">
    Check the accounting information and add any additional amounts as needed. The amounts shown are based on when tickets were paid for and do not include upsells.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_16178505-da29-44a3-a67d-bfc1f8a31be4.gif" alt="Accounting" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review commission and fees">
    Review the commission splits and issuing fees set up by your agency, then click **Submit**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_09e36173-fe5e-439a-8f2d-766d716d2472.gif" alt="Commission and fees" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update meals and seating">
    Click **Mass Update Flight Options** to review and update meals and seating.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_78de20e7-f17a-433b-a394-249048790582.gif" alt="Meals and seating" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update and preview the PDF">
    Once everything is correct, click **Update** - the PDF preview reflects your changes.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_a7b08070-7d76-4b12-8325-dd41d20377cf.gif" alt="PDF preview" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Associate payments">
    In trip details, scroll to **Payment History** and click **Add Transaction**. Associate a payment from an existing proposal or add a manual payment, then **Submit**. Revenue and expected profit adjust automatically.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_d0c97c2c-77fa-41c5-b1ec-a8d13e900c4b.gif" alt="Associate payments" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add a booking to an existing trip

<iframe
  src="https://play.gumlet.io/embed/6a5ebdb4e4e7b5ab4684ff36"
  style="width:100%; aspect-ratio:2/1; border:0; border-radius:12px; margin:0.75rem 0;"
  loading="lazy" allow="autoplay; encrypted-media; picture-in-picture; fullscreen"
  title="Video tutorial"
></iframe>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy trip details from the GDS">
    In the PNR, click **Show All** to expand every section, then highlight and copy the relevant text.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_d88332fa-e388-4be3-b284-8529143597c1.gif" alt="Copy details" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste into a new trip">
    Open a new trip in the CRM and paste the copied text.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_71a171d3-acdb-44f7-8631-7386296433cb.gif" alt="Paste details" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm flights and financials">
    Verify the flights, booking agency, and sales agents; check that tickets are assigned to the correct flights and that all financials, including upsells, are accurate.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_924e8c82-3d99-4496-993e-37092e1207c5.gif" alt="Confirm financials" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add bookings to the existing trip">
    After confirming commissions and fees, click **Add Bookings to Existing Trip** and select the trip from the drop-down. The booking is now merged into the existing CRM trip.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_b3056f48-5cc1-4536-84a5-50d5ca5fbbc2.gif" alt="Select existing trip" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Award, third-party, and manual trips are all supported, including marking a manual segment as **open** for reporting.
</Callout>
