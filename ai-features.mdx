AI runs throughout Yaalago to remove manual data entry. It is trained on travel-industry documents, and every extraction is reviewable before you finalize.

## What AI does

## More AI capabilities

- **Smart data parsing** - understands travel-industry formats and terminology; identifies passenger names, flight details, booking references, and pricing from unstructured or messy text.
- **AI flight option conversion** - paste the **text contents** of a flight option from Google Flights, Kayak, or Skyscanner (not a URL or screenshot) and get a branded proposal plus a GDS longsell code.
- **Multiple PDF import** - upload several PDFs at once and convert them together into a trip or proposal.

## The AI principles

<Callout kind="info">
