---
title: Checkout Pages
description: Every proposal can include a branded public checkout page where clients review the trip and pay via Stripe or USAePay.
---

Yaalago proposals come with **beautiful, branded checkout pages** - a public page with your logo, the trip details, itemized pricing, and a payment step. The client pays through the proposal, with no separate invoicing step required.

## What the client sees

- Your agency logo and branding
- Trip details and itemized pricing
- Your [terms and conditions](/setup/terms-conditions) embedded in the payment flow
- A secure payment step via **Stripe** or **USAePay**

## Enable checkout

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect a processor" icon="credit-card">
    Make sure Stripe or USAePay is connected in [Payment Processors](/setup/payment-processors).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your collection option" icon="sliders">
    On the proposal, decide whether to collect card details, show amounts only, or hide amounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the link" icon="send">
    Send the proposal - the client opens the checkout page and pays.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  Payments collected at checkout flow into [Payments](/financials/payments) and can be associated with the trip automatically.
</Callout>
