---
title: Proposal Builder
description: Turn any confirmation - GDS, PDF, email, or ITA Matrix - into a polished, branded client proposal with pricing, options, and a checkout page.
---

The **Proposal Builder** turns almost any source into a professional, client-facing proposal. You can set pricing, an expiry (default 24 hours, customizable with timezone), fare rules, and notes; add alternative [service options](/proposals/service-options); and choose whether to collect card details, show amounts only, or hide amounts for corporate clients. Only confirmed segments show in the client-facing output.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Proposals">
    Go to the proposals section and press the **plus sign**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter proposal details">
    Enter a clear name for the proposal and add the flight information.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_9bc3e6a0-bacf-48d2-83d5-c95e6c9f354b.gif" alt="Proposal details" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Specify payment options">
    Choose the payment option for this booking and group it by group or booking.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_43c273bd-0db2-4c39-bc2f-77ebeea415c0.gif" alt="Payment options" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your PNR">
    At the bottom of the page, add the **PNR** and convert.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_4ac283dd-4530-4a88-9393-ee3d51181bf9.gif" alt="Add PNR" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Input client information">
    Add the client's details, including email address and phone number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter costs and dates">
    Add the cost per adult and child, the time zone, and the expiration date.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_90df63a9-aacb-4504-a550-94dc78e5d76c.gif" alt="Costs and dates" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose refund and change types">
    Select the refund type and change type, and enter the **PCC** if applicable.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_444d48c2-e892-43d8-8d33-e1b5292800c2.gif" alt="Refund and change types" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add terms and notes">
    Include terms and conditions and any additional notes for the proposal.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_3ee3817b-2dde-4e5f-83be-b9b104f7f8ed.gif" alt="Terms and notes" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and share">
    Once saved, share the proposal via email, **Copy Short Link**, or a screenshot.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_f24a93bd-f565-4dc2-a69f-c94e1ea618b2.gif" alt="Share proposal" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit and add options">
    Go back anytime to edit the proposal and add options such as flights, car rentals, hotels, and more.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_6f6ecc44-f2db-429b-8f23-2b16da2805c4.gif" alt="Edit proposal" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Proposals can be edited anytime, and split, merged, or modified as the trip changes. For award trips, see [Booking with Points](/points/bookings).
</Callout>
