The **Proposal Builder** turns almost any source into a professional, client-facing proposal. Paste a GDS code, drop a PDF or email, or import an ITA Matrix itinerary, and AI structures it into a branded proposal - no manual entry.

## Build a proposal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a proposal" icon="file-plus">
    Create a new proposal and provide your source: a GDS code, a confirmation PDF or email, or an ITA Matrix itinerary.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set pricing and details" icon="dollar-sign">
    Add pricing, an expiry (default 24 hours, customizable with timezone), fare rules, and notes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add options" icon="list-plus">
    Include alternative itineraries and add-ons - see [Service Options](/proposals/service-options).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share" icon="send">
    Send the proposal with a branded [checkout page](/proposals/checkout) so the client can pay directly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Payment collection options

Choose how pricing is presented per proposal:

- **Collect card details** - client pays through the checkout page.
- **Show amounts only** - display pricing without collecting payment.
- **Hide amounts** - useful for corporate clients where the traveler should not see cost.

## Flexible editing

Proposals can be edited at any time, and you can **split, merge, and modify** them as the trip changes.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Only confirmed segments show in the client-facing output, so your proposals stay clean even when the underlying PNR has held or unconfirmed segments.
</Callout>
