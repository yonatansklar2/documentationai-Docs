---
title: Universal Confirmation Import
description: Drop any PDF, email, or screenshot from any provider worldwide and let AI extract the booking into a proposal or trip.
---

**Universal Confirmation Import** brings in bookings from any provider in the world - not just your GDS. Drop a PDF, email, or screenshot (airlines, hotels, cruises, tours, car rentals) and AI extracts the details in seconds. Every extraction is reviewable before you finalize. You can add an AI-imported booking to a proposal, bring in a non-GDS booking, or import an ITA Matrix itinerary.

## Add an AI booking to a proposal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Toggle on the proposal section">
    In **AI Booking**, toggle on the proposal section, then add your agency and the **booking type**.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_3adefe20-6aeb-4e0c-83ee-4bea8786fc15.gif" alt="Toggle proposal" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the confirmation">
    Paste the **confirmation text** or upload a **PDF**.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_44197582-407b-4206-8404-a7be104609c4.gif" alt="Enter confirmation" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the details">
    Review everything for accuracy, check that the **amounts** make sense, and save.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_43357d2b-0ccd-4a62-b67c-987850400ba2.gif" alt="Verify details" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to a proposal">
    Add it to a **new** or **existing** proposal. For a new proposal, enter a name and submit.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_775477e5-fe72-4f11-a5b8-9cbd2320c20d.gif" alt="Add to proposal" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share with your client">
    The booking lands on a proposal you can share with your customer to review and pay.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_b7922854-c858-4d64-b345-6c17c2fce83a.gif" alt="Share proposal" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Import a non-GDS booking

<Video
  src="https://play.gumlet.io/embed/6a5ebda0a151fd52184c4d0f"
  title="Import a non-GDS booking - video tutorial"
  width="960" height="480" allow-full-screen="true"
  style="width: 100%; max-width: 960px; height: auto; border-radius: 12px;"
/>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Booking">
    From the menu, select **AI Booking**.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_cd9329ef-7528-443e-808f-1af2705fc80d.gif" alt="AI Booking" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select agency and vendor">
    Choose whether to add the booking to a trip or a proposal, then select your agency and vendor. If the vendor isn't listed, click the **plus icon**, add its details, submit, and refresh. Select the booking type.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_a03bd046-69c2-4ed7-8708-1c8126c8072d.gif" alt="Select vendor" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the booking">
    Paste the booking text or upload a PDF.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_1fe708cb-0ad5-494c-ad96-bd6838fb3fc4.gif" alt="Enter booking" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and enrich">
    Open the booking to review it and confirm the details. You can add hotel photos or other information you want the client to see.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_b3e0443c-f6b5-4a5c-a05d-cb00488985d1.gif" alt="Review and enrich" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save to a trip">
    Save the booking to a new or existing trip. For a new trip, enter a name, select the booking agent, and submit.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_0f4815f8-22d5-4af0-a888-ac79d46c5774.gif" alt="Save to trip" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Import from ITA Matrix

<Video
  src="https://play.gumlet.io/embed/6a5ebdaee4e7b5ab4684fec2"
  title="Import from ITA Matrix - video tutorial"
  width="960" height="480" allow-full-screen="true"
  style="width: 100%; max-width: 960px; height: auto; border-radius: 12px;"
/>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the itinerary as JSON">
    In **Matrix**, select your trip and click **Copy itinerary as JSON file** on the right. Using the JSON gives a more accurate fare class than copying the result directly.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_7903bbf8-cfff-4038-a17f-f638e7008d60.gif" alt="Copy JSON" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open AI Booking in Yaalago">
    In Yaalago, go to **AI Booking**.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_88dd65dc-65d8-4034-9ba4-f9ffc54fafca.gif" alt="AI Booking" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select proposal and paste">
    Choose **Proposal** as the booking type and the flight type, then paste the Matrix JSON into the field.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_3be71daa-74e4-4b4d-b6a3-1af57b358745.gif" alt="Paste JSON" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import and process">
    Click **Import** and let the CRM process the search. Your trip details appear in Yaalago.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_6b953217-bd25-4ebd-b7af-c97258d1aa08.gif" alt="Import" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and edit">
    The original Matrix price is shown per traveler. Click **Edit** to adjust amounts per adult or child as needed.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_b421b35e-e9a4-427f-8997-de9348116bb1.gif" alt="Review and edit" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and save">
    Submit and save to a new or existing proposal. The Matrix data can also feed the **Add Flight GDS** function for graphic or text proposals, or the Add Trip function to create longsells for the GDS.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_d7943d2c-7e94-4ac0-812a-fa1cac1c9bf9.gif" alt="Submit and save" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  For online flight options from search sites, paste the option's **text** into [Build Longsell](/gds/build-longsell) or [Display Flights](/gds/display-flights) instead.
</Callout>
