**Universal Confirmation Import** lets you bring in bookings from any provider in the world - not just your GDS. Drop a PDF, email, or screenshot and AI reads it, extracts the details, and turns it into a proposal or a trip.

## Supported sources

Airlines, hotels, cruise lines, tour operators, car rentals - virtually any confirmation format:

- PDF confirmations
- Emails
- Screenshots
- GDS codes
- ITA Matrix itineraries

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload your confirmation" icon="file-input">
    Drop one or more files. Multiple PDFs can be uploaded at once, with vendor selection per booking type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI extracts the details" icon="sparkles">
    Yaalago reads passenger names, flight details, booking references, and pricing - even from messy or incomplete data - in seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and finalize" icon="check">
    Every extraction is reviewable and editable. Turn it into a proposal or import it as a trip.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  This is what makes Yaalago work for agencies that never touch a GDS - points brokers, concierge shops, and anyone working from provider PDFs. See also [Booking with Points](/points/bookings).
</Callout>

<Callout kind="info">
  For online flight options from search sites (Kayak, Google Flights, Skyscanner), paste the **text contents** of the option into [Build Longsell](/gds/build-longsell) or [Display Flights](/gds/display-flights) instead.
</Callout>
