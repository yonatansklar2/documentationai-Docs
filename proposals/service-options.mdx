---
title: Service Options
description: Add alternative itineraries and add-ons - hotels, bags, protection, car rentals, cruises, and private jets - within a single proposal.
---

A proposal is not limited to one itinerary. Add multiple service options so clients can compare and choose.

## What you can add

- Alternative flight itineraries
- Hotels
- Baggage
- Travel protection
- Car rentals
- Cruises
- Private jets

## Add an option

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the proposal" icon="file-text">
    Edit the proposal you are building.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a service option" icon="list-plus">
    Add an alternative itinerary or add-on with its own pricing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arrange and save" icon="save">
    Order the options as you want the client to see them, then save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Presenting two or three clearly priced options in one proposal makes it easy for clients to say yes quickly.
</Callout>
