---
title: Booking with Points
description: Turn a points booking into a trip, apply points from an account, and send a branded confirmation - with monitoring included.
---

Yaalago treats a points booking like any other trip, with the Points Vault handling the cost and profit side.

## From booking to branded output

<Steps>
  <Step title="Bring in the booking" icon="file-input">
    Import the confirmation via [Universal Confirmation Import](/proposals/confirmation-import) or enter it manually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply points from an account" icon="gem">
    Apply the points used from the relevant account. The [ledger](/points/ledger) decrements and the barter revenue is recorded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize and send" icon="send">
    Send the booking with your own logo and minimal branding, as if booked in your own system. Customize bags and weight (for example when a traveler has status granting extra bags).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Proposals for award trips

Build professional proposals for award and points trips that clearly show the points used, taxes, and any card charges. See [Proposal Builder](/proposals/overview).

<Callout kind="info">
  Points and non-GDS bookings still get automatic [schedule-change and cancellation monitoring](/trips/merge-monitoring) - so your agency is notified of airline changes even without a GDS PNR.
</Callout>
