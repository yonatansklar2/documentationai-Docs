The **Points Vault** is Yaalago's home for the points-and-miles side of your business. It stores loyalty accounts, credentials, and cards, and - crucially - tracks the **cost and profit** of every points balance so award bookings show real margin alongside cash trips. It is **free on every plan**.

## The three roles

The module is built on three roles:

| Role | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Seller** | The broker you buy points or miles from. |
| **Holder** | The person whose loyalty account you access to make the booking. |
| **Account** | A top-down view of your entire portfolio of points and miles. |

## What you can store

- Account login **credentials** and access notes
- How many times you bought points from a given seller, and **how much you paid**
- Whether points are **"clean" or "dirty,"** whether **ID** is needed, and whether you hold the account holder's info to make calls
- The **cost** of each balance and what it has **earned**

<Callout kind="tip">
  Because Yaalago knows what each points balance cost you, award bookings show real profit - not just a fuzzy "free flight."
</Callout>
