---
title: Users & Roles
description: Add agents to your agency and control what each can see and do with role-based permissions.
---

Yaalago supports multiple users with role-based permissions: **Admin** (full access), **Agent** (day-to-day work), **Super Admin**, and **View Only** (read access). Multi-user is available on Core, Pro, and Enterprise; Free allows up to 3 users and Solo is a single seat.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings">
    Navigate to the **Settings** page within the CRM.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_99507aee-7647-4a15-a547-a34a64c38614.gif" alt="Open settings" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Agency">
    In the **Agency** menu, select the agency where you want to add the agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Add Agent and role">
    Click **Add Agent**, then choose the role: **Admin**, **Agent**, **Super Admin**, or **View Only**.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_ae5aa1f1-d352-42d3-acb4-ef35113836b3.gif" alt="Add agent and role" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter agent details">
    Add the agent's name, phone number, username, and email. Optionally upload a logo, select a custom signature, and set a commission percentage.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_7254c055-497c-402f-8235-44c76479a31c.gif" alt="Agent details" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the user">
    Click **Submit** to save the new user in the CRM.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  On Core and Pro, seats beyond the 3 included are $25/mo each. See [Plans & Pricing](/pricing).
</Callout>
