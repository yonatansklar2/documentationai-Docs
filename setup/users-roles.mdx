---
title: Users & Roles
description: Add agents to your Yaalago agency and control what each can see and do with role-based permissions.
---

Yaalago supports multiple users with role-based permissions so your team can work together while you control access. Multi-user support is available on Core, Pro, and Enterprise (Solo is a single seat; Free allows up to 3 users).

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | Full access - agency settings, users, vendors, financials, and all trips and clients. |
| **Agent** | Day-to-day work - create clients, trips, proposals, and invoices; view their own performance. |
| **View Only** | Read access without the ability to change records. |

## Add a user

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open user settings" icon="users">
    Go to **Settings > Users** and click **Add User**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their details" icon="user-plus">
    Add the agent's name and email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a role" icon="shield">
    Choose Admin, Agent, or View Only. They receive an invitation to set their password and log in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  On Core and Pro, seats beyond the 3 included are $25/mo each. See [Plans & Pricing](/pricing).
</Callout>
