---
title: Transaction Modes & Excluded Emails
description: Customize transaction type labels and prevent internal emails from appearing on client profiles.
---

Two smaller settings that keep your data clean and your reporting accurate.

## Transaction modes

Transaction modes let you customize the predefined payment and transaction type labels so they match how your agency describes money movement.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open transaction modes" icon="sliders">
    Go to **Settings > Transaction Modes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the labels" icon="edit">
    Rename or adjust the predefined transaction type labels to fit your workflow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Excluded emails

Excluded emails prevent your own agency or host-agency email addresses from being picked up and attached to client profiles when trips and confirmations are parsed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open excluded emails" icon="mail-x">
    Go to **Settings > Excluded Emails**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add addresses to exclude" icon="user-x">
    Add your agency and host-agency addresses so they never appear as client contacts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Setting excluded emails early keeps your client database free of internal addresses as it grows.
</Callout>
