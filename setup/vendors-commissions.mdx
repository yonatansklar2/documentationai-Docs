---
title: Vendors & Commission Structures
description: Add the vendors you work with and define commission structures so profit, loss, and payouts are calculated automatically.
---

Vendors and their commission structures are the backbone of accurate reporting in Yaalago. Once configured, every booking knows exactly how much commission the agency and agent should expect.

## Add a vendor

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open vendor settings" icon="briefcase">
    Go to **Settings > Vendors** and click **Add Vendor**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter vendor details" icon="file-text">
    Add the vendor name and any relevant details. Vendors can be airlines, consolidators, hotels, cruise lines, tour operators, and more.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Commission structures

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a structure" icon="percent">
    For each vendor, define how commission is calculated - a percentage, a flat amount, or a more complex arrangement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set agent splits" icon="users">
    Define how commission is split between the agency and the agent so payouts are tracked automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Diverse commission structures per vendor are supported - set them up once and every future booking with that vendor inherits the math. This feeds directly into [Commission Tracking](/financials/commissions) and profit/loss per trip.
</Callout>
