---
title: Email & Text Templates
description: Customize the predefined email templates and prefilled client-facing text used across invoices, proposals, and confirmations.
---

Yaalago ships with predefined templates for the emails and text your clients see. Customizing them once keeps every communication on-brand and in your voice.

## Email templates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open templates" icon="mail">
    Go to **Settings > Templates** (or **Configure Profile Emails, Templates and Defaults**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit per document type" icon="edit">
    Customize the templates used for invoices, proposals, and trip confirmations.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Prefilled text templates

Prefilled text templates control the client-facing language your agency uses across the product - so agents do not rewrite the same phrasing every time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open prefilled text" icon="type">
    Go to **Settings > Prefilled Text Templates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize the wording" icon="edit">
    Adjust the default phrasing to match your agency's tone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Also review your [Terms & Conditions](/setup/terms-conditions) so they are embedded correctly in proposals and invoices.
</Callout>
