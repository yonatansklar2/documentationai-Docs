title: Email & Text Templates

---

Yaalago ships with predefined templates for the emails and text your clients see. Customizing them once keeps every communication on-brand. The first walkthrough covers your profile, permissions, email templates, and trip defaults; the second covers agency layout settings like email, name, and logo.

## Profile, templates & defaults

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Profile">
    In the navigation bar, select **Profile**. Here you can view your agency name, phone number, and username, adjust your clock display (12 or 24 hour), and update your password.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_e1297a42-fcdb-44b5-89a6-a527c71032d9.gif" alt="Profile" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
    
    The **Permissions** section shows everyone who currently has access to your account.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_04e54eb6-ec11-4307-a241-355b219e7ede.gif" alt="Permissions" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an employee">
    Click **Add Employee** and fill in their role type, name, address, phone, username, email, signature (if applicable), and commission percentage. Set their status to **Active** and choose their clock format.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_3984960c-7dc1-4110-b4eb-1f8fc1264812.gif" alt="Add employee" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit email templates">
    Open **Email Templates** to find customizable templates used throughout Yaalago - flight reminders, invoices, payment reviews, and transaction receipts. Toggle any template active or inactive, and click the **pencil icon** to edit its content, including the email body.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_2ac8b2b4-eb71-45eb-8199-6298931d2e69.gif" alt="Email templates" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_dc81d504-603e-4ae3-97c5-d249879e28c4.gif" alt="Edit template body" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set trip defaults">
    In **Trip Defaults**, configure what appears on confirmations: **cabin class** (defaults to B), **number of bags**, **weight unit** (pounds or kilograms), and **total weight**.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_13125a0e-7acb-42ab-8ec4-44ca06b4966b.gif" alt="Trip defaults" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Layouts & agency settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Edit the agency">
    In the navigation bar select **Agency**, then click the **pencil icon** to edit. Here you can adjust the account email, name, and logo, and choose the layout for your trip confirmations (a standard chronological itinerary style, or an alternative layout).
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_7cead092-8725-4c0c-aafd-4c238367dded.gif" alt="Confirmation layouts" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a default layout">
    Choose any layout as your default, then click **Save**. You can update it anytime.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_86517a6f-deb7-44a0-9fd2-a5cf3db211ee.gif" alt="Default layout" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
</Steps>
