## Agency profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open agency settings" icon="settings">
    Go to **Settings > Agency**. This is where your company identity lives.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add name, logo, and contact details" icon="building">
    Enter your agency name, upload your logo, and add your address and contact info. These appear on all client-facing documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your default GDS" icon="plane">
    Choose whether your agency works in **Sabre only**, **Amadeus only**, or **both**. This sets the default output format for GDS tools; you can still switch per action.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Recommended setup order

For a clean start, configure these in order - each one makes the next step more accurate:

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="1. Vendors & Commissions" icon="briefcase" href="/setup/vendors-commissions">
    Add vendors (with PCCs, void rules, and time zones) and commission structures so reporting is accurate from the first trip.
  </Card>
  <Card title="2. Users & Roles" icon="users" href="/setup/users-roles">
    Add agents and assign Admin, Agent, or View Only permissions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="3. Payment Processors" icon="credit-card" href="/setup/payment-processors">
    Connect Stripe and/or USAePay to collect payments.
  </Card>
  <Card title="4. Templates & Terms" icon="mail" href="/setup/templates">
    Customize email templates, trip defaults, and terms.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Set up vendors and commission structures before importing lots of trips - it means your profit/loss and commission numbers are right from the first booking. While you're in Settings, you can also enable [Sabre Sync](/gds/sabre-sync) so bookings mirror in automatically.
</Callout>
