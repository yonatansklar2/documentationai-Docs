---
title: Payment Processors
description: Connect Stripe and USAePay to collect card payments through Yaalago checkout pages and invoices.
---

Yaalago collects client payments through your own processor - **Stripe** or **USAePay**. Connecting one enables checkout pages on proposals and payment links on invoices, and you can set a default when you connect more than one.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Payment Details">
    Navigate to **Settings > Agency**, select the agency you want to adjust, and click **Payment Details**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_031f5844-07c0-4ad6-8482-52d83cfc14ad.gif" alt="Open payment details" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable a Payment Provider">
    Enable either **Stripe** or **USAePay** as your payment provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Stripe or USAePay Keys">
    For **Stripe**, paste the secret key generated in your Stripe account. For **USAePay**, enter the source key along with its PIN.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_23f13269-cbe2-4fc5-9d54-e726f574ce22.gif" alt="Enter keys" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Payment Settings">
    Click **Submit** to save your payment settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Default Payment Option">
    Select the **Default Payment** option for proposals as needed.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_3e853452-870a-4510-9297-1a4fec9dbfac.gif" alt="Default payment option" />
    </Frame>

    Proposals can collect payment by Stripe, collect card details for use within the GDS, charge through your own credit processor, confirm with amounts shown, or confirm with amounts hidden.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  Payments collected here flow into [Payments](/financials/payments) and associate with trips and invoices automatically.
</Callout>
