---
title: Payment Processors
description: Connect Stripe and USAePay to collect card payments through Yaalago checkout pages and invoices.
---

Yaalago collects client payments through your own payment processor. Two are supported: **Stripe** and **USAePay**. Connecting one enables checkout pages on proposals and payment links on invoices.

## Connect Stripe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open payment settings" icon="credit-card">
    Go to **Settings > Payment Methods**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your Stripe key" icon="key">
    Paste your Stripe API secret key. Stripe supports card payments and Apple Pay, with automated reminders and fee accounting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a default" icon="check">
    If you connect more than one processor, choose which is used by default.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connect USAePay

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open payment settings" icon="credit-card">
    Go to **Settings > Payment Methods**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add USAePay credentials" icon="key">
    Enter your USAePay credentials as an alternative US payment processor.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  Payments collected through Stripe or USAePay flow into [Payments](/financials/payments) and can be associated with trips and invoices automatically.
</Callout>
