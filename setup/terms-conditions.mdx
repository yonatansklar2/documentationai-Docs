---
title: Terms & Conditions
description: Edit and customize the terms and conditions embedded in your invoices and proposals.
---

Your terms and conditions are embedded directly into the payment flow on invoices and proposals, so clients agree to them as they pay. They appear across confirmations, invoices, and proposals.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Agency">
    In the navigation bar, select **Agency**.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_79d79885-b20c-46e4-8a7f-3f73d30e6619.gif" alt="Select agency" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit Agency">
    Click the **pencil icon** to edit your agency.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_d998e28f-7a84-4e77-aa0d-56228602a859.gif" alt="Edit agency" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access Settings">
    Open the **Settings** section to find all the terms and conditions.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_631a7218-9da2-49a4-b169-1318d6bfcfdf.gif" alt="Settings section" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust invoice terms">
    Adjust the terms and conditions that show up on **invoices**.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_39b717f4-f1a3-4f08-8f1f-341339acbedb.gif" alt="Invoice terms" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add fare rules">
    Add terms and fare rules in link form so the customer sees the fare rules for their booking.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_0ff197aa-e310-4447-989e-7361f7e0c6aa.gif" alt="Fare rules" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize notes">
    Add custom notes shown at the bottom, plus important notices in the general terms and conditions.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_6e3c4b39-7b9c-4a23-a60b-1a5903901d79.gif" alt="Custom notes" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust formats">
    Set additional formats including date format, currency, and default weight units.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_b1674e8d-0be8-408a-b6a2-67375d4b6fc5.gif" alt="Formats" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set flight reminder emails">
    Configure the automatic 36-hour pre-flight reminder. Best practice: set it to **Agency only** first to review timing and content, then switch to **Agency and Clients** to send externally. Click **Submit** to save all changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  Terms appear on the [checkout pages](/proposals/checkout) clients use to review and pay. See also [Pre-Journey Reminders](/trips/reminders).
</Callout>
