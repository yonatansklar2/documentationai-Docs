---
title: Terms & Conditions
description: Edit and customize the terms and conditions embedded in your invoices and proposals.
---

Your terms and conditions are embedded directly into the payment flow on invoices and proposals, so clients agree to them as they pay.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open terms settings" icon="file-check">
    Go to **Settings > Terms and Conditions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your terms" icon="edit">
    Customize the language for your agency. These terms appear on invoices and proposals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save" icon="save">
    Save your changes - they apply to new client-facing documents going forward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  Terms are part of the checkout experience - see [Checkout Pages](/proposals/checkout) for how clients review and pay.
</Callout>
