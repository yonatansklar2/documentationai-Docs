## Overview

Start managing your travel projects with Yaalago in a few quick steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign In">
    ````
    Go to `https://app.yaalago.com` and sign in to your account.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Project">
    ````
    Click "New Project" in the dashboard.
    Enter a project name and optional description.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Team">
    ````
    Invite others via email or share the project link.
    Start documenting together.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>
