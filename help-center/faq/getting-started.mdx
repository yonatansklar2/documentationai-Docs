## Overview

Yaalago helps you organize all your travel project documentation in one place.

## Steps to Begin

1. Visit `https://app.yaalago.com` and create a free account.
2. Confirm your email address.
3. Log in and click "New Project."
4. Enter your project details and invite your team members.

## Tips

Use the navigation sidebar to access your docs, settings, and collaboration tools.
