---
title: Getting Started FAQ
description: Answers to common questions about starting with Yaalago - pricing, GDS support, points, and data.
---

<Expandable title="Do I need a credit card to start?">
  No. Yaalago's Free plan has no time limit and requires no credit card. Start at [yaalago.com](https://yaalago.com).
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Which GDS does Yaalago support?">
  Both **Sabre** and **Amadeus**, natively. You can set a default in [agency settings](/setup/agency) and convert between them with cross-GDS [Build Longsell](/gds/build-longsell).
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I use Yaalago without a GDS?">
  Yes. With [Universal Confirmation Import](/proposals/confirmation-import) and the [Points Vault](/points/overview), agencies that work only with points or provider PDFs can run entirely in Yaalago.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="How do I get my existing clients in?">
  Bulk import them via CSV, including preferences, frequent flyer numbers, and passports. See [Import & Export](/clients/import-export).
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Is the Points Vault an add-on?">
  No - the [Points Vault](/points/overview) is free on every plan.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="How do I collect payment from clients?">
  Connect [Stripe or USAePay](/setup/payment-processors), then use branded [checkout pages](/proposals/checkout) on proposals or payment links on [invoices](/financials/invoices).
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I export my data?">
  Yes - CSV export of client data is always available. See [Import & Export](/clients/import-export).
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="How do I add my team?">
  Add agents with roles (Admin, Agent, View Only) in [Users & Roles](/setup/users-roles). Multi-user is available on Core, Pro, and Enterprise.
</Expandable>
