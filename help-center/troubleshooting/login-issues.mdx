---
title: Login Issues
description: Troubleshoot problems signing in to Yaalago.
---

Having trouble signing in at [crm.yaalago.com/dashboard](https://crm.yaalago.com/dashboard)? Work through these steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your verification email" icon="mail">
    If you just signed up, confirm your email via the link sent to your inbox. Check your spam folder if it has not arrived.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your email and password" icon="key">
    Make sure you are using the email you registered with. Use **Forgot password** to reset if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try another browser" icon="globe">
    Yaalago works in Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari. Clear your cache or try a different browser if a page will not load.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your admin" icon="users">
    If your agency admin added you, confirm your account was activated and your role assigned. See [Users & Roles](/setup/users-roles).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  Still stuck? Email [support@yaalago.com](mailto:support@yaalago.com) and include the email address you use to sign in.
</Callout>
