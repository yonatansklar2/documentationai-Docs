---
title: Can't Log In to Yaalago
description: Fix login problems and access your Yaalago account.
---

## Symptoms

- Forgot password error
- No login email received

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reset Your Password">
    ````
    Go to `https://app.yaalago.com/forgot-password` and enter your email.
    Check your inbox for the reset link (including spam folder).
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact Support">
    If the email doesn't arrive, email `support@yaalago.com` for assistance.
  </Step>
</Steps>
