Yaalago uses a five-tier model. **Start free - no time limit, no credit card required.** Paying annually gives you **2 months free** on Core and Pro.

## Plans at a glance

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Users | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | - | Up to 3 | Getting started with real platform access |
| **Solo** | $50/mo | $500/yr | 1 user | Independent advisors who want the full toolkit |
| **Core** | $99/mo | $990/yr | 3 incl., +$25/seat | Small teams needing a multi-user CRM |
| **Pro** (most popular) | $199/mo | $1,990/yr | 3 incl., +$25/seat | Growing agencies - everything Core + Solo |
| **Enterprise** | Contact Sales | Contact Sales | Unlimited | Larger orgs needing SSO, white-label, SLA |

<Callout kind="info">
  Additional seats are $25/mo on Core and Pro. Solo is a single-seat plan; if you need more users, move up to Core or Pro.
</Callout>

## What's included

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Free">
    Generous usage caps with real platform access: GDS conversion (Sabre + Amadeus), cross-GDS longsell, trip + client management, AI booking imports (modest quota), universal confirmation parsing, branded confirmations, 36-hour pre-flight reminders, multi-currency support, **Points Vault**, **GDS Sync** (free during beta), and community support.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Solo">
    Full feature parity with Pro for a single seat. Adds over Free: proposal builder, client checkout (Stripe + USAePay), invoicing, financial tools + reporting, commission tracking, document management, email support, and higher usage quotas. *"As you grow, we grow with you."*
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Core">
    Team CRM without billing tools. Adds over Free: 3 users, multi-user roles and permissions, subagency management, and higher team-level quotas. Proposals, invoicing, and financial tools are **not** included - that is the upgrade path to Pro.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Pro">
    Everything Solo has plus everything Core has. Adds over Core: proposal builder, client checkout, invoicing, financial tools + reporting, commission tracking and splits, document management, and priority email support.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Enterprise">
    Unlimited users, custom feature development, a dedicated account manager, custom API integrations, SSO and advanced security, custom workflows and automations, white-label options, an SLA, 24/7 priority support, and custom training.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="tip">
  **Points Vault is free on every tier** - it is Yaalago's home for the points-and-miles segment, with no per-feature add-on.
</Callout>

See live pricing and start at [yaalago.com/pricing](https://yaalago.com/pricing).
