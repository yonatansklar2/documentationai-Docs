Yaalago connects to the systems travel agencies already use.

## GDS

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Sabre" icon="plane">
    Native connection: PNR import, real-time updates, passenger extraction, queue management, and void-period monitoring. Real-time sync via [Sabre Sync](/gds/sabre-sync).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Amadeus" icon="plane">
    Native connection: automated reservation import, multi-segment support, commission tracking, and void-period monitoring.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Payments

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Stripe" icon="credit-card" href="/setup/payment-processors">
    Card payments and Apple Pay, automated reminders, and fee accounting. Connect via your Stripe API secret key.
  </Card>
  <Card title="USAePay" icon="credit-card" href="/setup/payment-processors">
    Alternative US payment processor; add your credentials in agency settings.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Browser extension

<Card title="Yaalago Porter" icon="chrome" href="/gds/porter">
  Free companion extension for Chrome and Firefox: one-click PNR capture from Sabre Red Web and Amadeus Selling Platform Connect, "bring over this page" capture from any booking site, and GDS sign-in autofill. Local-only and private.
</Card>

<Callout kind="info">
  Document management is available on paid tiers (Basic on Solo/Core, Advanced on Pro, Unlimited on Enterprise). Enterprise also supports custom API integrations.
</Callout>
