## Welcome to Yaalago

**Yaalago** is a Travel Agency CRM and back-office platform built for modern travel agencies - whether you book on **Sabre** or **Amadeus**, work with **points and miles**, or run multi-vendor trips with any provider in the world. It covers the full agency workflow in one place: client management, GDS shortcuts, proposals, trip management, invoicing, commission tracking, task automation, and financial reporting.

> "The CRM built for modern travel agencies. Manage clients, automate bookings, track commissions, and create stunning proposals - all in one place."

Yaalago was built by the team at **Emes Travel**, a working travel agency that spent years (and tens of thousands of dollars) searching for software that did everything they needed before building their own. It replaces the 10-15 open tabs, spreadsheet tracking, and manual GDS entry that most agencies live with.
