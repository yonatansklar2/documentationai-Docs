---
title: Merging Duplicate Clients
description: Review and combine duplicate client records side by side, keeping the most complete data.
---

Over time you may accumulate duplicate client records. The **Duplicate Client Merge** tool lets you clean them up with a side-by-side review.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open duplicate merge" icon="git-merge">
    From the clients area, open the duplicate merge tool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare side by side" icon="columns">
    Yaalago shows the candidate records next to each other and auto-selects the most robust data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Merge in one click" icon="check">
    Confirm to merge into a single clean record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  This is the manual cleanup tool. Yaalago also **automatically** creates and merges client records whenever a new trip comes in - see [Client Database](/clients/overview).
</Callout>
