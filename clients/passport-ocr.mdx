---
title: Passport OCR & AI Upload
description: Upload a passport image and let AI extract the details and populate the client record automatically.
---

Upload any passport image - even a phone photo - and Yaalago's AI extracts the name, date of birth, nationality, passport number, and issue and expiry dates, then populates the client record.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload the passport" icon="scan-line">
    From Prep Travelers, the clients list, an existing client page, or the longsell flow, upload the passport image.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the extraction" icon="eye">
    AI fills in the fields. Every extraction is reviewable and editable before you save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save to CRM" icon="save">
    Save the details to the client record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- **Multiple entry points** - upload from wherever you happen to be in the workflow.
- **Multi-passport support** - dual-passport holders can have each passport associated with specific segments at longsell time.
- **Expiry reminder** - if the passport expires more than 6 months out, Yaalago offers to create a 6-month-ahead expiry reminder on save.

<Callout kind="tip">
  AI extractions are always reviewable - Yaalago is fast, but you stay in control of what gets saved.
</Callout>
