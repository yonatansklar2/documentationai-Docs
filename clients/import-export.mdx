---
title: Import & Export (CSV)
description: Bulk import your existing clients - with preferences, frequent flyer numbers, and passports - and export your client data at any time.
---

Moving to Yaalago from another system, or need your data out? CSV import and export are both fully supported.

## Import clients

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare your CSV" icon="file-spreadsheet">
    Include client details along with preferences, frequent flyer numbers, and passport information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the file" icon="upload">
    From the clients area, choose **Import** and upload your CSV.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map and confirm" icon="check">
    Confirm the fields map correctly, then import. Your clients appear in the database.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Export clients

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open export" icon="download">
    From the clients area, choose **Export**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the CSV" icon="file-down">
    Yaalago generates a CSV of your client data to download.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  Your data is yours - export is always available.
</Callout>
