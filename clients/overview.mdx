Yaalago keeps every client in one centralized database - and it largely builds itself as you work.

## What a client profile holds

- Contact details and travel history
- Passports (with expiry tracking) and TSA Pre-Check
- Frequent flyer numbers and loyalty programs
- Meal and seat preferences
- Tags, groups, and relationships to other travelers

## Automatic client creation and merge

Whenever a trip enters Yaalago - via **Sabre Sync**, a **manual Sabre or Amadeus PNR paste**, or a **proposal built from any source** - Yaalago automatically creates a client record from the parsed traveler details. If those details match an existing client, the records are **automatically merged** (name + date of birth / email / phone matching). No duplicates, no manual entry.

<Callout kind="info">
  This automatic merge runs on every incoming trip. It is separate from the manual [Duplicate Client Merge](/clients/merge) tool, which you use to review and clean up existing duplicates side by side.
</Callout>
