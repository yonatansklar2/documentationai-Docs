Yaalago gives you three distinct ways to organize and connect records. They look similar but do different jobs.

## The three tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Client tags** | Segment and connect clients (for example `VIP`, `Corporate`, `Family`) and use them for targeted outreach. |
| **Invoice tags** | Group invoices by event or company for reporting. |
| **Relationships** | Link passengers together so that when you longsell one, Yaalago asks whether to include the others. |

## Tag a client

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a client" icon="contact">
    Go to the client profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a tag" icon="tags">
    Apply an existing tag or create a new one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Link travelers with relationships

<Callout kind="tip">
  Relationships are essential for families flying together - set them once and Yaalago prompts you to include the whole party at [Build Longsell](/gds/build-longsell) time, so nobody gets left off the booking.
</Callout>
