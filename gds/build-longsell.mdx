---

**Build Longsell** is Yaalago's headline GDS tool. It takes almost any input - a stored client record, a Sabre or Amadeus PNR, a PNR from a different PCC, or the pasted text of an online flight option - and outputs a **longsell code** in your choice of Sabre or Amadeus, copied to your clipboard. Cross-GDS conversion (Sabre PNR to Amadeus longsell, or vice versa) works in one step, and a per-segment **NN/GK toggle** controls how segments are sent to the GDS.
