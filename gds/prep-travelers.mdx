---
title: Prep Travelers
description: Paste a list of travelers and instantly get correctly formatted passenger codes in Sabre or Amadeus, with automatic age-status detection.
---

**Prep Travelers** (formerly Convert PAX) turns a plain list of travelers into correctly formatted passenger codes for your GDS - no manual typing of name formats. It detects age status automatically (lap infants, children, adults), handles compound surnames with the `=` separator, and can pull details straight from stored client profiles.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Prep Travelers">
    In the shortcuts menu, navigate to the **Prep Travelers** section and add your clients.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_aK1YKLprgC.jpeg" alt="Prep Travelers" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust Client Details">
    For each client, specify their gender (male or female) and passenger type (adult or child).
    
    **View available formats** - below the client details, you'll see all the available formats for the conversion, including frequent flyer formats and email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Passport Image">
    If needed, upload the client's passport image in the designated field.
    
    **Automatic passport expiry reminder** - once a passport is uploaded, the system offers an email reminder six months before it expires, so you never miss a renewal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust Settings and Convert">
    Adjust any remaining settings, then click **Convert and Copy**.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_U3XQPwwlDz.jpeg" alt="Convert and copy" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
    
    The information is copied to your clipboard, ready to paste into Sabre or Amadeus. You can also save the converted client to your **CRM** or attach it to an existing **trip**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Uploading a passport here also offers a 6-month-ahead expiry reminder on save. See [Passport OCR & AI Upload](/clients/passport-ocr).
</Callout>
