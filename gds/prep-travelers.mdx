**Prep Travelers** (formerly Convert PAX) turns a plain list of travelers into correctly formatted passenger codes for your GDS - no manual typing of name formats.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Prep Travelers" icon="user-check">
    From the GDS tools, choose **Prep Travelers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste your travelers" icon="clipboard">
    Paste the list of names (or pull them from stored client records).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Sabre or Amadeus" icon="plane">
    Pick your output format. Amadeus output supports configurable name order and date format.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the codes" icon="copy">
    Yaalago outputs the passenger codes, ready to paste into your GDS.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What it handles automatically

- **Age-status detection** - lap infants, children, and adults are identified automatically.
- **Multiple last names** - compound surnames are handled using the `=` separator.
- **Bassinets and special cases** - common passenger scenarios are supported.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Passenger details can also be pulled from stored [client profiles](/clients/overview), including passports and loyalty numbers, so you rarely retype anything.
</Callout>
