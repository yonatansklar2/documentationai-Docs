## Sabre vs Amadeus

Yaalago outputs in whichever GDS you choose. Set your agency default in [Set Up Your Agency](/setup/agency), and switch per action when you need the other format. Cross-GDS conversion means you can take a **Sabre PNR and get an Amadeus longsell** (or vice versa) in one step.
