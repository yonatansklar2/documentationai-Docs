title: Sabre Sync
description: Automatically pull bookings from your Master PCCs into Yaalago and keep them in sync in real time.
---

**Sabre Sync** connects your Sabre PCCs to Yaalago so bookings flow in automatically and stay up to date - no manual PNR pasting for synced PCCs. GDS Sync is **free during beta**.

## Request access

Sabre Sync requires a one-time setup and approval.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the GDS Connection Panel" icon="refresh-cw">
    As an agency admin, open the **GDS Connection Panel** in settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your Master PCCs" icon="list-checks">
    Choose the Master PCCs you want to sync.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for approval" icon="clock">
    A system admin reviews and approves the request. Once approved, your bookings begin syncing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you get

- Automatic PNR import from your synced PCCs
- Real-time updates as bookings change
- Automatic [schedule-change and cancellation monitoring](/trips/merge-monitoring)

<Callout kind="info">
  Expanded GDS Sync - including repricing tools and alerts when specific unwaitlistable segments become available in Sabre and Amadeus - is in active development.
</Callout>
