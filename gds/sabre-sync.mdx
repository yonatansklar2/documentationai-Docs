title: Sabre Sync

**Sabre Sync** connects your Sabre PCCs to Yaalago so bookings flow in automatically and stay up to date - no manual PNR pasting for synced PCCs. GDS Sync is **free during beta**.

<Callout kind="info">
  **New feature.** Sabre Sync connects your Yaalago agency to Sabre GDS, letting you import PNRs directly into trips with a single click - no copy-pasting, no manual entry. About 5 minutes to set up; agency admin required.
</Callout>

**Before you begin:** you need **agency admin access** and at least one active Sabre PCC (Pseudo City Code). If you're unsure of your PCC codes, contact your Sabre account manager.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Agency Settings">
    From your Yaalago dashboard, click your **profile avatar** in the top-right corner, then select **GDS Connection Panel**. (Alternatively, go to **Settings > Agency** and open the Settings tab inside Edit Agency.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request GDS access">
    Click **Request Access** on the right of the Settings tab. In the modal, select **Sabre** as your GDS (Amadeus is also available), check each **Master PCC** you want to connect (e.g. 4B8K, AZ4K, IH6K, M85L), then click **Submit Request**. Don't see your PCC? Type it into the *Request a new one* field and press Enter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for approval">
    Yaalago's system administrator reviews the request. Once approved, the GDS bot is activated for your agency and you'll get a notification when it's ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import a PNR from the GDS">
    Go to **Trips > Add Trip** and click **Import From GDS**. Select your **PCC** from the dropdown, enter the **PNR code** (e.g. ABCD12), and click **Import**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the GDS bot to fetch the PNR">
    The GDS bot fetches the booking data from Sabre in the background. Watch the bell icon; when the notification reads **PNR import complete - Done**, click the *Pending trips (GDS)* link to go to the review queue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and convert the pending trip">
    In **Pending Trips (GDS)**, find the imported booking row (Agency, PNR, PCC, Source, flights, client, ticket number). Use the row actions: **Convert to Trip**, **Re-fetch** (pull fresh data), **View raw data**, or **Delete**. On **Convert**, Yaalago pre-fills the trip form with all flight, client, and ticket data; review the date verification dialog and click **Apply Changes** to finalize.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  Every flight in Yaalago - synced or not - is automatically monitored for [schedule changes and cancellations](/trips/merge-monitoring). Expanded GDS Sync (repricing and unwaitlistable-segment alerts) is in active development.
</Callout>
