---
title: Display Flights
description: Generate a screenshot-ready, branded visual flight option to send directly to clients - no more screenshotting your GDS terminal.
---

**Display Flights** takes the same inputs as [Build Longsell](/gds/build-longsell) - a Sabre or Amadeus PNR, or the pasted text of an online flight option - but the output is a clean, branded visual flight option designed to send straight to a client.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Paste in your PNR">
    Head to **Display Flights** and paste in the PNR. You can adjust the number of passengers and the price per person.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_P1vizOXW9F.gif" alt="Paste PNR" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust settings">
    Adjust the settings according to your needs.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_355hIvSuO1.gif" alt="Adjust settings" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Graphical View">
    Select **Graphical View** to see the options presented with names and details.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_LAIJlJo9YF.gif" alt="Graphical view" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit notes">
    Edit the notes by double-clicking and changing them as needed.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_Q_XbO73T5r.gif" alt="Edit notes" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy or download">
    Copy the image, download it, or take a screenshot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the text version">
    Switch to the text version for a simpler option to send by email or WhatsApp.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_z1UO2B8pNZv.gif" alt="Text version" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  For online options, paste the **text contents** of the option (airline, flight numbers, times, price) - not a URL or screenshot.
</Callout>
