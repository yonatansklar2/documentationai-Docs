---
title: Display Flights
description: Generate a screenshot-ready, branded visual flight option to send directly to clients - no more screenshotting your GDS terminal.
---

**Display Flights** takes the same inputs as [Build Longsell](/gds/build-longsell), but instead of a GDS code the output is a **screenshot-ready visual flight option** designed to send straight to a client. No more screenshotting your GDS terminal or formatting flight times in a Word doc.

## Inputs and output

| Input | Output |
|---|---|
| A **Sabre or Amadeus PNR** | A branded visual flight option |
| The **pasted text of an online flight option** (Kayak, Google Flights, Skyscanner) | A branded visual flight option with the same look |

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Display Flights" icon="image">
    Choose **Display Flights** from the GDS tools.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide your input" icon="clipboard">
    Paste a PNR or the text contents of an online flight option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the visual" icon="sparkles">
    Yaalago produces a clean, branded flight option ready to send to your client.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  For online options, paste the **text contents** of the option (not a URL or screenshot) - the same input mechanic as Build Longsell.
</Callout>
