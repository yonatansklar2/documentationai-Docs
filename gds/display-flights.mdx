---
title: Display Flights
description: Generate a screenshot-ready, branded visual flight option to send directly to clients - no more screenshotting your GDS terminal.
---

**Display Flights** takes the same inputs as [Build Longsell](/gds/build-longsell) - a Sabre or Amadeus PNR, or the pasted text of an online flight option - but the output is a clean, branded visual flight option designed to send straight to a client.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Paste In Your PNR">
    Head over to **Display Flights** and paste in the PNR. You can adjust the number of passengers and the price per person.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_P1vizOXW9F.gif" alt="Paste PNR" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust Settings">
    Adjust the settings according to your needs.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_355hIvSuO1.gif" alt="Adjust settings" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Graphical View">
    Select **Graphical View** to see the options presented with the names and the options below.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_LAIJlJo9YF.gif" alt="Graphical view" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit Notes">
    Edit the notes by double-clicking and changing them as needed.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_Q_XbO73T5r.gif" alt="Edit notes" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy or Download">
    You can either copy the image, download it, or take a screenshot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Text Version">
    Head over to the text version for a simpler option for email or WhatsApp.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_z1UO2B8pNZv.gif" alt="Text version" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  For online options, paste the **text contents** of the option (airline, flight numbers, times, price) - not a URL or screenshot.
</Callout>
