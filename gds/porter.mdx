**Yaalago Porter** is a free browser extension that speeds up the handoff between the web and Yaalago. It is available on **Chrome** and **Firefox**.

## What it does

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="One-click PNR capture" icon="clipboard-check">
    Capture a PNR from Sabre Red Web or Amadeus Selling Platform Connect, cleaned to plain text on your clipboard.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bring over this page" icon="download">
    Copy any booking site (hotels, cars, cruises, private jets, flights, tours) as clean text and images to feed Yaalago's AI booking import.
  </Card>
  <Card title="GDS sign-in autofill" icon="key">
    Fill your saved GDS sign-in and enter the authenticator code.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Local and private" icon="shield">
    Local-only: no server, no tracking, nothing leaves your browser.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Install

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your browser" icon="chrome">
    Visit the [Browser Extension page](https://yaalago.com/browser-extension) on the Yaalago site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to Chrome or Firefox" icon="download">
    Install from the Chrome Web Store or the Firefox Add-ons store.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and go" icon="log-in">
    Open a GDS or booking site and use the Porter buttons to capture and send content into Yaalago.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Porter pairs naturally with [Build Longsell](/gds/build-longsell) and [Universal Confirmation Import](/proposals/confirmation-import) - capture on one tab, build in Yaalago on the next.
</Callout>
