Yaalago turns your booking and payment data into reports you can actually use.

## Custom financial reports

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open reports" icon="bar-chart-3">
    Go to the reporting area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter" icon="filter">
    Filter by agency, agent, or client.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export" icon="download">
    Export to CSV or PDF.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Multi-currency

<Callout kind="info">
  Yaalago handles GBP, AUD, EUR, and other currencies natively, so agencies working across markets keep accurate financials in the right currency.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Analytics on sales volume, revenue, agent performance, and commissions are available on Pro and above - see [Dashboard & Analytics](/dashboard).
</Callout>
