title: Payments
description: Record and associate payments, route payments across agencies and vendors, and keep a full audit trail of money movement.
---

Yaalago records every payment and ties it to the right trip and invoice, giving you a complete financial picture per booking.

## Record a payment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the trip" icon="map">
    Go to the Edit Trip page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the payment" icon="banknote">
    Record the payment - card payments via Stripe/USAePay flow in automatically, and manual/offline payments can be entered too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Associate it" icon="link">
    Associate the payment with the trip so balances and profit/loss update.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Payment routing

Split payments across agencies and vendors, and handle transferring an issued ticket into the CRM after a Stripe or USAePay payment.

## Audit trail

<Callout kind="info">
  Payment history gives you a full audit trail of all money movement, including manual transactions for offline payments and adjustments.
</Callout>
