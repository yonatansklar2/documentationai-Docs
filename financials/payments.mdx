---
title: Payments
description: Record and associate payments to trips, route payments across agencies and vendors, and keep a full audit trail.
---

Yaalago records every payment and ties it to the right trip and invoice. Card payments via Stripe or USAePay flow in automatically, and manual or offline payments can be entered too. The walkthrough below covers associating payments to a trip.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a payment transaction">
    Scroll down to **Payment History** and click **Add Transaction**.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_20fce6d0-e4de-46b9-b2c9-acc7f6b300d0.gif" alt="Add transaction" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Associate or add a payment">
    Associate a payment from an existing proposal, or add a manual payment using credit transaction modes and credit card payments.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_d4519f40-cf21-4a3c-93e1-3a56ee35febf.gif" alt="Associate payment" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit payments">
    Click **Submit** to add the payments. Revenue and expected profit update automatically to reflect received payments.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_97b2b51b-6db1-46f2-af8c-08f6cc465d44.gif" alt="Submit payments" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  Payment history gives a full audit trail of all money movement, including payment routing across agencies and vendors.
</Callout>
