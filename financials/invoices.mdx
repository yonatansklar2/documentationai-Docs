title: Invoices
description: Create invoices from a trip or from scratch, send them with a payment link, and edit them even after full payment.
---

Yaalago's invoicing is integrated with trips and payments, so your billing and your bookings stay in sync.

## Create an invoice

There are several ways to create one:

- **From a trip** - auto-build an invoice from the trip record
- **From scratch** - build a custom invoice with your own line items
- **Via the + icon** - quick-create from anywhere

You can also attach trips to new or existing invoices.

## Send an invoice

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the invoice" icon="receipt">
    Add custom line items and confirm pricing and markup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send" icon="send">
    Send with an optional message to one or more recipients, with a PDF download and a payment link (Stripe or USAePay).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Partial payments

Track outstanding balances on partially paid trips and request additional funds. Add partially paid trips to an invoice to keep the balance accurate.

## Edit after payment

<Callout kind="info">
  You can edit invoices even after they are fully paid - Yaalago auto-recalculates totals and syncs the associated trips and records.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="tip">
  When you update a trip's price, sync it with the associated invoice so both always match.
</Callout>
