---
title: Invoices
description: Create invoices from a trip or from scratch, send them with a payment link, and manage or edit them - even after full payment.
---

Yaalago's invoicing is integrated with trips and payments. Create an invoice from a trip, from scratch, or via the + icon; add custom line items; and send with a PDF and a payment link (Stripe or USAePay). You can edit invoices even after full payment - totals recalculate and associated trips and records stay in sync.

## Create and send an invoice

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an invoice">
    To stack several options onto one invoice for clients who pay later, either use the **plus icon** to create one directly, or open the trip and create an invoice from it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to a master invoice">
    With the trip selected, go to the **Action** tab and add it to a new or existing master invoice.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_b4ba76e4-ef5e-44b0-82dc-f63fd377c590.gif" alt="Add to invoice" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select tickets">
    Choose an existing or new invoice, then **Select All** tickets or pick them manually.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_9dfbe9f9-2942-4e68-aa78-4c00b8e142ac.gif" alt="Select tickets" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and select flights">
    Submit the invoice, then make sure to select **flights** and submit.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_d6921e21-1b8e-42d8-a474-b47bca9b3248.gif" alt="Select flights" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter invoice details">
    Enter an invoice name and the client it is for, set the due date, group by booking or ticket, and add any notes.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_40e1f72c-2c9b-4c53-b625-690aaaf1bea7.gif" alt="Invoice details" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set payment options and save">
    Select the payment options you want to accept, review, and **Save**.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_f92fa548-85a1-494d-86fd-4a56bf610766.gif" alt="Payment options" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the invoice">
    Once saved, share it by copying a link or sending it by email.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_1820d569-06ad-474c-a6e5-7aa4ecf71fb1.gif" alt="Share invoice" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage your invoices

<Video
  src="https://play.gumlet.io/embed/6a5ebda3a151fd52184c4d59"
  title="Manage your invoices - video tutorial"
  width="960" height="480" allow-full-screen="true"
  style="width: 100%; max-width: 960px; height: auto; border-radius: 12px;"
/>

To view your invoices, go to the **Invoice** tab, where you'll find a list of all created invoices.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter invoices">
    Select the type of invoice you'd like to see, and filter by specific clients.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_65e2d2aa-35d0-45e4-9c92-5a18fc07cf12.gif" alt="Filter invoices" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit an invoice">
    Open a specific invoice to edit it. You can change the payment method and toggle whether remaining amounts are shown.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_6c055027-846a-4a1a-b379-c3bf0e11a98a.gif" alt="Edit invoice" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit items and upsell">
    Edit the flights and bookings on the invoice and add more via **Add Items**. You can upsell ticket amounts (for example +$50 across all tickets), then submit.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_74d63dc8-77da-46c5-a545-fd78b944f8ee.gif" alt="Upsell" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a transaction">
    Add a transaction to mark an invoice (or one of its items) as paid.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_d4a9cca7-f0fe-4dd9-9bce-d84055998145.gif" alt="Add transaction" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Save your changes to update the invoice.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_1c6332c1-cee9-488d-a80f-9de130f00fd9.gif" alt="Save changes" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Track outstanding balances on partially paid trips and request additional funds when needed.
</Callout>
