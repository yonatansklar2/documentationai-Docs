Yaalago gives you one unified view of your agency, with deeper analytics as you grow.

## Centralized dashboard

See upcoming bookings, commission earnings, payment statuses, and active tasks - all in one place.

## Calendar view

<Callout kind="tip">
  The calendar view puts every trip, deadline, and departure at a glance, so nothing sneaks up on you.
</Callout>

## Analytics

On Pro and above, the analytics dashboard covers:

- Sales volume and revenue
- Agent performance
- Commission tracking

## Enterprise automations

<Callout kind="info">
  Enterprise agencies can add custom API integrations and automation flows tailored to their specific workflows. See [Plans & Pricing](/pricing).
</Callout>
