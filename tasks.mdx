---
title: Tasks & Reminders
description: Create tasks tied to trips and segments, set email reminders, and let Yaalago handle deadlines automatically.
---

Yaalago's task system keeps your agency on top of everything around a trip. Tasks tie to specific trips and flight segments, use agency-defined categories, and can send email reminders (for example 24 hours before a flight). Reminders are timezone-aware, linked tasks update when a flight changes, and passport-expiry tasks are created automatically 6 months ahead.
