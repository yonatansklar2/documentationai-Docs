---
title: Tasks & Reminders
description: Create tasks tied to trips and segments, set email reminders, and let Yaalago handle deadlines automatically.
---

Yaalago's task system keeps your agency on top of everything around a trip. Tasks tie to specific trips and flight segments, use agency-defined categories, and can send email reminders (for example 24 hours before a flight). Reminders are timezone-aware, linked tasks update when a flight changes, and passport-expiry tasks are created automatically 6 months ahead.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find tasks fast">
    Use the filters to sort tasks by upcoming, current, or past, or narrow by agent or keyword search.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_Dbgp_gi35_.gif" alt="Filter tasks" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and link a task">
    Select your agency and link the task to a flight. Assign it to an agent, choose the task type, and set a deadline.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_NWw9cvsszL.gif" alt="Create task" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Flag urgent tasks">
    Mark a task as urgent and trigger a confirmation email as a built-in reminder.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot__m06ED7vFe.gif" alt="Urgent task" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tag and describe">
    Tag the relevant employee and add a description so they have everything they need.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_B-_R1fBdnFQ.gif" alt="Tag employee" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    When everything looks good, hit **Submit**.
    
    <Image src="https://kb-assets.31.97.159.229.sslip.io/snapshot_7dgRjfo86QE.gif" alt="Submit task" width="1200" height="600" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px;" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  The task dashboard shows all upcoming tasks across every trip in one view - filter it to focus on what is due now.
</Callout>
