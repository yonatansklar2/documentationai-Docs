A summary of notable Yaalago releases. For the full, live list and what's coming next, see the [public roadmap](https://roadmap.yaalago.com).

<Update label="2026" description="Points & award bookings">
  ## Points Vault & award ledger

  The [Points Vault](/points/overview) module lets you store loyalty accounts, credentials, and cards, and track cost and profit per account with a full [ledger and barter math](/points/ledger). Free on every plan.
</Update>

<Update label="2026" description="GDS tools">
  ## Display Flights & Sabre Sync

  - **[Display Flights](/gds/display-flights)** - generate screenshot-ready, branded visual flight options from a PNR or a pasted online option.
  - **[Sabre Sync](/gds/sabre-sync)** - automatically pull bookings from your Master PCCs, kept in sync in real time (free during beta).
  - **[Yaalago Porter](/gds/porter)** - the browser extension is now available on both Chrome and Firefox.
</Update>

<Update label="2026" description="Trips & monitoring">
  ## Change monitoring on every flight

  - Any flight entered into Yaalago - via AI booking or manual entry, not only GDS Sync - is now [monitored for schedule changes and cancellations](/trips/merge-monitoring).
  - Merged trips show both agency (PNR) and airline confirmation codes per passenger, everywhere.
  - Trip timeline ordering places hotels, car pickups, and flights in the correct order.
</Update>

<Update label="2026" description="AI & proposals">
  ## AI flight option conversion & multi-PDF import

  - Paste the text of an online flight option (Google Flights, Kayak, Skyscanner) to get a branded proposal plus a GDS longsell code.
  - Upload multiple PDFs at once for AI import, with vendor selection per booking type.
  - Per-trip toggle to hide fare class on confirmation emails.
</Update>

<Update label="2026" description="Invoicing">
  ## Edit fully paid invoices

  Edit invoices even after full payment - Yaalago auto-recalculates totals and syncs the associated trips and records. See [Invoices](/financials/invoices).
</Update>
