## Prerequisites

Before starting, ensure you have:

<Callout kind="info">
- A valid email address for account creation
- Access to your travel agency's details (name, address, branding)
- Optional: Your GDS credentials if integrating PNR parsing
</Callout>

<Callout kind="tip">
No credit card required for the free trial. Upgrade anytime from the dashboard.
</Callout>

## Create and Access Your Account

Follow these steps to sign up and log in to Yaalago.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up" icon="user-plus">
    Visit [https://yaalago.com](https://yaalago.com) and click **Sign Up**.

    Fill in your email, agency name, and create a strong password.

    Verify your email via the confirmation link sent to your inbox.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Log In" icon="log-in">
    Go to [https://app.yaalago.com/login](https://app.yaalago.com/login).

    Enter your credentials or use Google SSO if enabled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Browser" icon="globe">
    Use Chrome, Firefox, or Safari for the best experience.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Mobile App" icon="smartphone">
    Download the Yaalago app from the App Store or Google Play for on-the-go access.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Configure Your Dashboard

Customize your centralized dashboard for bookings, tasks, and finances.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete Profile" icon="settings">
    Navigate to **Settings {arrow-right} Agency Profile**.

    Add your agency logo, address, and time zone.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Up Integrations" icon="plug">
    In **Integrations**, connect your GDS (Amadeus, Sabre) or payment provider.

    Copy your API key from the provider dashboard and paste it here.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Customize Views" icon="layout">
    Go to **Dashboard {arrow-right} Customize**.

    Drag widgets for commissions, upcoming bookings, and client tasks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add Your First Client Profile

Client profiles store contacts, documents, tags, and booking history.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Profile" icon="user">
    Click **Clients {arrow-right} New Client**.

    Enter name, email, phone, and travel preferences.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Details" icon="file-text">
    Upload passport/docs and apply tags like `VIP` or `Family`.

    Merge duplicates if needed via the search bar.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save and Tag" icon="save">
    Hit **Save**. The profile appears in your dashboard client list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<ParamField path="clients" param-type="object" required="true">
  Base endpoint for client management: `{https://api.yaalago.com/v1/clients}`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="name" param-type="string" required="true">
  Client full name.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="email" param-type="string" required="false">
  Contact email for automated follow-ups.
</ParamField>

## Verify Setup with API (Optional)

Test your setup by fetching your first client via API.

<CodeGroup tabs="JavaScript,Python,cURL">
  ```javascript
  const response = await fetch('https://api.yaalago.com/v1/clients', {
    headers: {
      'Authorization': 'Bearer YOUR_API_KEY',
      'Content-Type': 'application/json'
    }
  });
  const clients = await response.json();
  console.log(clients);
  ```

  ```python
  import requests

  headers = {
      'Authorization': 'Bearer YOUR_API_KEY',
      'Content-Type': 'application/json'
  }
  response = requests.get('https://api.yaalago.com/v1/clients', headers=headers)
  clients = response.json()
  print(clients)
  ```

  ```bash
  curl -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
       -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
       https://api.yaalago.com/v1/clients
  ```
</CodeGroup>
