title: Quickstart
description: Create your Yaalago account, set up your agency, and import your first trip in about 15 minutes.
---

This guide takes you from a brand-new account to your first imported trip. No credit card is required - the Free plan has no time limit.

## 1. Create your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up" icon="user-plus">
    Go to [yaalago.com](https://yaalago.com) and click **Get Started**. Enter your email, agency name, and a password, then verify your email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in" icon="log-in">
    Sign in at [crm.yaalago.com/dashboard](https://crm.yaalago.com/dashboard). Yaalago runs in your browser - Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari - and is fully mobile-responsive.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 2. Set up your agency

<Steps>
  <Step title="Agency profile" icon="building">
    Add your agency name, logo, and contact details so proposals, invoices, and confirmations are branded. See [Set Up Your Agency](/setup/agency).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your GDS" icon="plane">
    Set your default GDS - Sabre, Amadeus, or both. See [Users & Roles](/setup/users-roles) and the [GDS overview](/gds/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add vendors" icon="briefcase">
    Add the vendors you work with and their commission structures so reporting is accurate from day one. See [Vendors & Commission Structures](/setup/vendors-commissions).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 3. Import your first trip

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="From Sabre" icon="plane">
    In Sabre, display the full PNR, copy it, then in Yaalago click **Add Trip** and paste. Yaalago parses passengers, segments, and fares automatically. See [Importing Trips](/trips/overview).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="From Amadeus" icon="plane">
    Copy the Amadeus PNR and paste it into **Add Trip**. See [Importing Trips](/trips/overview).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="From a PDF / email" icon="file-text">
    Drop any confirmation PDF or email from any provider and let AI extract the details. See [Universal Confirmation Import](/proposals/confirmation-import).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="tip">
  When a trip is imported, Yaalago automatically creates a client record from the traveler details - and merges it if the client already exists. Your client database builds itself.
</Callout>

## 4. Send a proposal or invoice

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build a proposal" icon="file-plus">
    Turn the trip into a branded proposal with pricing and options, then share a public checkout link. See [Proposal Builder](/proposals/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invoice and collect" icon="receipt">
    Generate an invoice from the trip and collect payment via Stripe or USAePay. See [Invoices](/financials/invoices).
  </Step>
</Steps>
